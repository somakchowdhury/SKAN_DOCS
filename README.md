## 1. Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC)

### Role & Purpose
The Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) oversees human participant research, ensuring all protocols adhere to ethical standards, safeguard subject rights and privacy, and comply with regulatory requirements. Any study involving human subjects, samples, or data must receive prior IEC clearance.

## 2. Institutional Biosafety Committee (IBSC)

### Role & Purpose
The Institutional Biosafety Committee (IBSC) reviews and approves research involving recombinant DNA technology, biological agents, toxins, and potentially infectious materials. The IBSC ensures appropriate biosafety containment levels, risk assessment, and environmental handling procedures.

### Key Contact Persons
